THE PROBLEM

A lost season isn't the only option.

Whether you're sidelined by a meniscus issue, rotator cuff strain, tennis elbow, or ankle injury - or you're still active but know something is getting worse - the choice can feel binary: a long surgical timeline or simply living with it.

Being active isn't just something you do. It's part of who you are. An injury threatens that identity, not only your schedule.

YOUR PHYSICIAN GUIDE

We understand what it feels like to have a season taken away - and why you don't want to wait for a nagging issue to become a bigger one.

Your physician uses your imaging, activity goals, and injury history to build a return-to-activity or preventive plan grounded in your actual condition.

No. Cellaxys also evaluates active adults recovering from or trying to get ahead of sports injuries.
