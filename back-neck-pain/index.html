COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you make a decision.

I've tried everything. How is this different?+

The consultation begins with imaging and a physician assessment of the disc, joint, or structure that may be driving symptoms. It is a different question than symptom management alone.

Is this only for low-back pain?+

No. Cellaxys evaluates the full spine: cervical (neck), thoracic (mid-back), and lumbar (low-back).

Is my case severe enough - or too severe?+

Imaging and your medical history clarify candidacy. A consultation is an evaluation, not a commitment to treatment.
