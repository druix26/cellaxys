WHY CELLAXYS EXISTS

We know what it's like to be handed one option and no plan B.

Most patients arrive after seeing at least one other doctor. They've been told to get a replacement, try another round of PT, or simply live with it.

Cellaxys begins somewhere else: a real imaging review, an honest answer on candidacy, and - when it fits - a physician-led, non-surgical plan with a fraction of the downtime.
