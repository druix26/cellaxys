COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you make a decision.

I was told I'm bone on bone. Isn't surgery my only option?+

Not necessarily. A consultation and fresh imaging review - not a prior verbal diagnosis - determines candidacy. Cellaxys does not confirm or rule out candidacy on this page.

Is Stem Cell Therapy legitimate?+

Cellaxys care is physician-led and imaging-based. Your consultation covers what the procedure can and cannot realistically do for your specific knee.

Why consider self-pay care?+

The decision is personal. Many patients compare the investment with time away from work, rehabilitation, and the disruption associated with a surgical path. Financing options are available.
